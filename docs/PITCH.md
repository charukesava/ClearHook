## Tailor by Audience

### For Judges
Focus on: Problem + Demo + Why it matters to India

### For Investors
Focus on: Market size + Revenue model + Scalability

### For Users
Focus on: How it saves you + How easy it is

### For Tech Media
Focus on: Claude API usage + Real-time detection + Explainability

---

## Delivery Tips

✅ Say problem first (judges care)
✅ Show product second (they understand visually)
✅ Say why it matters (makes it memorable)
✅ Be specific ("₹2 crore" > "lots of losses")
✅ Don't apologize ("We built this weekend" not "Just a weekend project")
✅ Own the room (Confidence sells)

---

## Don't Say

❌ "It's like ChatGPT for phishing" (vague)
❌ "We'll disrupt cybersecurity" (too bold for hackathon)
❌ "It's AI-powered" (everyone says this)
❌ "We're solving the biggest problem" (hubris)
❌ "Sorry, it's just a demo" (undercuts your work)

---
